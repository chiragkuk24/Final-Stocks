How Accuracy is Calculated

Institutional Post-Market Protocol

Post-market accuracy validation is conducted automatically at 4:00 PM IST following market close. Every setup is evaluated against real-time exchange feeds to determine mathematical target hits and error bounds.

Criterion A: Range Envelope Containment
Actual Low ≥ (Pred Low × 0.985) &&
Actual High ≤ (Pred High × 1.025)
Validates whether intraday price action stayed safely above expected support floor while achieving predicted upside bounds.
Criterion B: Closing Price Tolerance
|Actual Close - Pred Close| / Pred Close ≤ 2.5%
Validates whether the 4:00 PM IST actual market closing price landed within 2.5% of the ML ensemble target.
Overall Accuracy Rate Formula
Overall Accuracy % = (Total Target Hits / Total Evaluated Setups) × 100
🎯 Target Hit = Criterion A OR Criterion B

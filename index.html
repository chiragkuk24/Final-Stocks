2. Machine Learning & Deep Learning Models Architecture

QUANTMATRIX AI employs a multi-model consensus strategy combining 11 state-of-the-art machine learning algorithms to eliminate individual model bias and capture both linear and non-linear market patterns.

1. PyTorch LSTM Neural Network
Deep Recurrent Neural Network with Long Short-Term Memory cells. Captures multi-day sequential dependencies, momentum inertia, and complex non-linear price trajectory curves.
Deep Learning Time-Series
2. Random Forest (Classifier & Regressor)
Ensemble of de-correlated decision trees constructed over randomized feature subsets. Reduces prediction variance and prevents overfitting on noisy intraday bars.
Tree Ensemble
3. XGBoost (Extreme Gradient Boosting)
Optimized regularized gradient boosting framework. Uses exact greedy algorithm splits with L1/L2 regularization to handle high-dimensional momentum inputs.
Gradient Boosting
4. LightGBM (Light Gradient Boosted Machine)
Leaf-wise tree growth engine providing rapid training speeds and exceptional accuracy on volatile price breakout features.
Fast Tree Boosting
5. Gradient Boosting Machine (GBM)
Sequential additive tree model that minimizes residual prediction loss iteratively, establishing strong baseline directional momentum predictions.
Additive Boosting
6. Extra Trees (Extremely Randomized Trees)
Randomizes cut-point choices when splitting tree nodes, significantly reducing structural variance caused by unexpected gap ups or downs.
Variance Reduction
7. AdaBoost (Adaptive Boosting)
Focuses sequentially on difficult, high-volatility trading setups by increasing weights on misclassified historical market regimes.
Adaptive Learner
8. Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP Neural Net)
Fully connected deep neural network with ReLU activation functions and Dropout regularization for non-linear pattern synthesis across multi-timeframe DMAs.
Artificial Neural Net
9. Support Vector Machine (SVM / SVR)
Constructs optimal decision hyperplanes with non-linear Radial Basis Function (RBF) kernels to isolate bullish momentum breakout zones.
Hyperplane Separator
10. L2 Regularized Ridge Classifier
Linear classification model with Tikhonov (L2) regularization. Prevents multi-collinearity issues across highly correlated technical indicators (RSI, MACD).
L2 Regularized
11. Logistic & Bayesian Probabilistic Meta-Scorer
Meta-ensemble calibrator that aggregates outputs from all 10 base models and scales them into a single, calibrated Win Probability percentage (0% to 100%).
Meta Calibrator

4. How Accuracy is Calculated (Post-Market Audit Protocol)

Every single prediction generated by the engine is automatically audited against official exchange closing prices at 4:00 PM IST following market close.

Criterion A: Directional Accuracy
(Actual Close - CMP) × (Pred Close - CMP) > 0
Validates whether the stock/index moved in the predicted bullish or bearish direction relative to morning CMP.
Criterion B: Closing Price Tolerance
|Actual Close - Pred Close| / Pred Close ≤ 2.5%
Validates whether the 4:00 PM IST actual market closing price landed within 2.5% tolerance of the predicted close.
Overall Accuracy Formula
Overall Accuracy % = (Total Target Hits / Total Setups Evaluated) × 100
🎯 Target Hit = Criterion A OR Criterion B

5. What You Need to Know Before Using This Dashboard

To maximize your trading efficiency and manage risk effectively, understand these core dashboard concepts:

Signal Conviction Levels
  • HIGH CONVICTION Win Prob ≥ 60%, EV ≥ +2.5%, Risk-Reward ≥ 1.5. Premium institutional setups.
  • MODERATE Win Prob 52% - 59%, positive EV. Standard breakout candidates.
  • AVOID Win Prob < 52% or negative EV. Non-favorable risk profiles.
Entry & Chase Price Rules
  • Buy Entry Range: Optimal price range to accumulate positions.
  • Max Chase Price: Strict execution boundary — NEVER buy above this level if stock gaps up.
  • Dynamic Stop Loss: ATR-calculated floor for systematic risk management.
Risk-to-Reward & EV %
  • Expected Value (EV %): Mathematical expectancy combining win probability and payout ratio.
  • Risk-to-Reward: Aim for setups with Risk-Reward ratio ≥ 1.5 : 1.
  • Position Sizing: Calculates exact shares based on 2% risk limit on ₹500,000 capital.
